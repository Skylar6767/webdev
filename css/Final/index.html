Bounty Eats


Best Taste Throughout the Wasteland!

After getting bored of staying home, Frederick Bulsara strived to make his life more exciting. Once he saw Jam's success he knew he had to make a restuarant. After he created the most delicious foods with the most delicious of ingredients, he had the ownership of an award-winning restuarant.

History!

On December 13th, 2187, after the G4 Summit was held, Asuka R. Kreutz removed the Flame Of Corruption from Sol Badguy, leaving him as his human self, Frederick Bulsara. Frederick then settled down with his wife, Jack-O'. The two of them had a hobby of creating just about anything, Frederick made a rocket ship in his own backyard. After a while of this calm life though, he decided he wanted to do something greater. After seeing Jam's (sort of) success at running a restuarant, Frederick decided he would run his own restuarant, calling it 'Bounty Eats'. The first Bounty Eats would open in Lars Canyon, USA.
